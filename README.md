## Main Features
<a id="main-features"></a>

### Core Functionality
- **Audio and MIDI Editing**: Effortlessly edit and sequence audio and MIDI tracks with precision, allowing for detailed customization of your compositions.
- **Unlimited Channels and Instruments**: Enjoy the flexibility of using unlimited channels and instruments, enabling complex arrangements without restrictions.
- **Drag and Drop Support**: Streamline your workflow with intuitive drag-and-drop functionality, making it easier to arrange and edit tracks.
- **Automatic Delay Compensation**: Ensure perfect timing in your mixes with the automatic delay compensation system, which adjusts for any latency issues in real-time.
- **Advanced Automation**: Utilize advanced automation tools to create dynamic mixes with ease, adjusting parameters over time for a polished sound.

### Advanced Capabilities
- **Presence Sampler Included**: Access the powerful Presence sampler to create unique sounds and textures, enhancing your music production experience.
- **MIDI Mapping System**: Take advantage of the intuitive MIDI mapping system to quickly assign controls and streamline your performance setup.
- **Real-time Time Stretching**: Change the tempo of your audio on the fly without affecting its pitch, providing flexibility in your arrangements.
- **Compatibility with ASIO and Core Audio**: Ensure high performance and low latency with wide compatibility across ASIO, Windows Audio, and Core Audio controllers.
